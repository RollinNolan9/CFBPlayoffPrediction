How the rankings work

The saved opponent-relative ridge model powers these rankings, using uncapped margins and no direct game-count predictors. Opponent-relative performance compares a team's past margins with opponents' results available before each game. The explicit preseason roster component is off; historical performance still informs other features. It is reused without retraining. Every team faces the full FBS field on a neutral site; both team orientations are evaluated and averaged to remove the model's home-label offset. The resulting ratings are centered at zero across the field. AP votes are not an input.
